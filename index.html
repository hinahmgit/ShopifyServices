Questions, answered

How long does a redesign take?

Most full redesigns take 2 to 4 weeks, depending on the number of pages and custom features. You'll get a clear timeline before we start.

Do you work with US clients across time zones?

Yes. I work with US brands and keep my schedule flexible. Most updates happen async, so nothing waits on time zones, and calls are booked at a time that suits you.

Do I need to be on Shopify already?

No. I can redesign an existing Shopify store, or set up a new one for you. If you're moving from another platform, let's talk about your setup first.

What does the audit include?

The free mini audit is a short recorded walkthrough of the top issues on your store. The full CRO audit covers your homepage, collections, product pages, cart and mobile experience, with a prioritised fix list. I use my own audit tool, UXAuditor, for heatmaps, accessibility and speed checks on desktop, tablet and mobile. The full audit is $100.

How do payments work?

Simple and fair: 50% upfront to book your project, and 50% at launch. Payment is by bank transfer. Every project starts with a clear written quote.

What do you need from me to start?

Your store link, collaborator access to Shopify, your logo and brand assets, and a short chat about your goals. I'll send a simple checklist so nothing is missed.
